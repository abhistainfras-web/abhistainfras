# AURA LUXE | Prime Global Real Estate & Sovereign Asset Advisory

An ultra-luxury, high-performance web platform crafted specifically for high-net-worth property buyers, family offices, and institutional real estate investors.

---

## ✨ Signature Features

- **Editorial Aesthetic & Modern Layout**:
  - Deep obsidian (`#06080a`) and warm champagne/gold palettes.
  - Serif typography with `Playfair Display` paired with `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
  - Glassmorphic panels, subtle backdrop blurs, and gold glow micro-interactions.

- **Cinematic Interactive Hero**:
  - Dynamic full-bleed estate showcase with smooth transitions and slide telemetry (`01 Skycrest Penthouse`, `02 Villa Palais de l’Océan`, `03 The Trousdale Monolith`).
  - Integrated luxury filter bar: Global Enclaves (Manhattan, French Riviera, Beverly Hills, Dubai, St. Moritz), Typology (Penthouses, Waterfront, Architectural, Alpine), and Investment Brackets.
  - Live transacted volume ticker: **$4.8B+**, **99.4% Discretion**, **18 Sovereign Hubs**.

- **Curated Trophy Portfolios**:
  - Interactive multi-image photo gallery on each property card.
  - Multi-currency live conversion (**USD $**, **EUR €**, **GBP £**, **AED د.إ**).
  - Private wishlist & shortlist manager saved across sessions via local persistence.
  - Fast category filters & sorting by Price, Yield/ROI, and Scale.

- **Interactive Investor ROI & Yield Engine**:
  - Real-time investment modeling for family offices.
  - Dynamic sliders for Acquisition Value ($5M - $100M+), Equity / Down Payment %, Projected Net Yield, and 5-Year Capital Appreciation.
  - Live projection of Net Annual Cash Flow, 5-Year Equity Growth, and Annualized Equity ROI.
  - Golden Visa and Sovereign Wealth Tax Shelter qualification notes.

- **Global Wealth Hubs Showcase**:
  - Spotlight on Monaco, Dubai Palm Jumeirah, Manhattan Billionaires’ Row, and St. Moritz.
  - Average price/sq.ft metrics and tax incentives.

- **Bespoke VIP Concierge & Confidential Off-Market Vault**:
  - Stealth off-market acquisitions, private jet & yacht logistics, and legal structuring.
  - Bilateral NDA and private client accreditation gateway.

- **Interactive Modals & Drawers**:
  - Comprehensive Property Dossier modal with photo gallery, room specs, architectural narrative, bespoke amenities, and CAD blueprint overview.
  - Discreet viewing scheduler with options for **In-Person Champagne Walk**, **Helicopter Aerial Flyover**, or **Encrypted Ultra-HD Live Stream**.
  - Sliding Shortlist Drawer with batch dossier request.
